WELCOME TO YOUR DESKTOP

AMBIENTCLOCK

An evolving collection of animated wallpaper clocks. The first scene is yours to choose.

01 / INSTALL

Open iTerm2

In the project directory, run the one-time installer:

bash install.sh

Then reload your shell: source ~/.zshrc

02 / CONNECT

Point Plash here

Add a Local Website in Plash and choose your AmbientClock repository's root folder (the one containing this page).

03 / EXPLORE

Pick a clock

Browse two curated collections from your terminal, or switch instantly:

clock switch

clock use forest
